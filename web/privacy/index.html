Privacy Policy — Zikr Vibe

Effective date: 2026-05-02 · Last updated: 2026-05-18

1. Who we are

Zikr Vibe (the "App") is operated by Soul Vibe Technology Limited (HK), a Hong Kong company ("we", "us", "our"). Contact: soulvibeai@gmail.com.

2. Privacy by design — what we explicitly do NOT collect

Your dhikr content stays on your device. We never collect, store, or transmit:

  • The specific dhikr you recite (Subhanallah, Alhamdulillah, etc.)
  • Your dhikr counts
  • Your daily targets
  • The duration of your prayer sessions

All of this stays on your device, in local storage only.

3. What we collect

We collect only the minimum data needed to run the App.

3.1 Account data

  • Email address (required for sign-in)
  • Display name (you choose)
  • Avatar URL (optional)
  • Authentication provider (email or Google)
  • Timezone (default Asia/Dubai, used for daily counters)
  • Prayer method preference (e.g. Umm Al-Qura, used for prayer time calculation)

3.2 Daily presence (✓/·)

If you choose to participate in the optional Circle (group streak) feature, we record one boolean per day per user: active or not active. We do not record what you did, how many times, or for how long.

3.3 Streak counters

Aggregate numbers only: current streak, longest streak, last active date, mercy days used. These are derived from your daily presence above.

3.4 Circles (groups)

If you create or join a Circle:

  • Circle name and description (you choose)
  • Invite code (random string, only Circle members can see)
  • Member count and shared streak

3.5 Notification preferences

Which prayer time reminders you have enabled (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) and your offset preference. Notifications are scheduled locally on your device — they do not originate from our server.

3.6 Skin purchase entitlements

The App offers optional cosmetic skin packs (each $1.99 USD). Payment is processed entirely by Google Play Billing: Google sees your payment method; we do not. We receive only a confirmation that a given skin SKU was purchased, which we store as a boolean entitlement on your account so the skin unlocks on every device you sign into. We do not store your card number, billing address, or transaction history.

4. How we use your data

  • To authenticate you and let you sign back in
  • To show your streak and your Circle's shared streak
  • To calculate prayer times for your location and method
  • To deliver the prayer reminders you opted in to

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or selling to third parties.

5. Where your data lives

  • On your device (phone or browser): all dhikr content, all counts, all targets, session durations, app preferences. We have no access to this data.
  • Our backend (Supabase): only the categories listed in Section 3 above. Access is gated by row-level security (each row tied to your user ID). No third-party trackers, analytics, or advertising SDKs.

6. Third parties

The App talks to a small number of third parties only when needed:

  • Supabase (hosted Postgres + Auth) — stores account data, daily presence, streaks, Circle data. Privacy policy: supabase.com/privacy
  • Aladhan Prayer Times API (api.aladhan.com) — when you request prayer times for a location, we send the latitude/longitude and your chosen calculation method. We do not send your name or email. Privacy policy: aladhan.com/privacy-policy
  • Google Sign-In (if you sign in with Google) — Google provides your email and basic profile per their terms. Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Billing (if you purchase a skin pack) — Google processes the payment and tells us only that a given SKU was purchased. We never see your card or billing address. Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

7. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with advertisers. We share data only:

  • With other members of a Circle you joined — only the data needed for the Circle (your display name, avatar, daily presence ✓/·, your streak number)
  • With service providers strictly for infrastructure (Supabase as our database host)
  • If required by law or legal process (court order, lawful request from authorities)

8. Data retention and deletion

Account data is kept while your account is active. To delete your account and all server-side data:

  • In-app: Settings → Delete Account
  • By email: send a request to soulvibeai@gmail.com from your registered email address; we will delete within 30 days

Deleting your account removes: profile, daily presence history, streaks, notification preferences, and Circle memberships.

On-device dhikr data is removed when you uninstall the App or clear browser storage. We cannot delete it remotely because we never had it.

9. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (GDPR for EU/EEA, PDPO for Hong Kong, CCPA for California, etc.), you may have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold on you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise any right, email soulvibeai@gmail.com.

10. Children

The App is intended for users 13 and over. If you are under 13 (or under 16 in EU/EEA), please do not create an account. If we learn we have collected data from a child without verifiable parental consent, we will delete it promptly.

11. International data transfers

We are based in Hong Kong. Our infrastructure providers (Supabase) may store data in the US or EU. By using the App, you consent to this transfer. We rely on Supabase's standard contractual clauses for transfers from EU/EEA.

12. Security

We use HTTPS for all data in transit, row-level security in our database (so users can only access their own data and what their Circle members have explicitly shared), and the principle of least privilege for backend access. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify you of any breach affecting your data within 72 hours of discovery, where required by law.

13. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be communicated in-app or by email.
