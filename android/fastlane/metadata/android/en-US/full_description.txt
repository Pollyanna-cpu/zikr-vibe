Your dhikr deserves better than ads and distractions.

Zikr Vibe is a distraction-free tasbih counter built for Muslims who want to remember Allah without their phone pulling them away. No ads. No data collection. No haram content interrupting your worship. Just tap, count, and feel.

DISTRACTION-FREE COUNTING
Open the app, tap the screen, close your eyes. The large tap zone fills your screen. Each tap gives gentle haptic feedback. When you reach 33, 66, or 99, a strong vibration tells you without looking.

33-BEAD PROGRESS
A ring of 33 dots shows exactly where you are in your current round of tasbih.

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
Your dhikr counts are stored on your device. We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not track your prayer habits. Privacy is not a feature — it is a right.

STREAK TRACKING
See your current streak of consecutive days with dhikr. No scores. No grades. Just consistency, on your terms.

DHIKR CIRCLE (Companionship, Not Competition)
Create a private dhikr circle with family or close friends. See who did their dhikr today — not how much, just that they showed up. No leaderboards. No rankings.

PRAYER TIMES & QIBLA
Accurate prayer times for your location. Built-in Qibla compass pointing toward Mecca.

Free. No ads. No in-app purchases. No tricks.
