## Landmines to Plant

Questions to seed in the buyer's mind that expose iQibla weaknesses:

1. **"Ask them where their company is actually headquartered."** Their answer will reveal the Dubai/Shenzhen disconnect. UMEOX is a Shenzhen ODM — the Dubai branding is marketing.

2. **"Ask what material the $39 model is made of."** They'll say plastic. Then compare to our metal + IPX7 at the same price.

3. **"Search '[iQibla] + your model name + review' before buying."** QC complaints surface immediately: connection drops, counting errors, button failures.

4. **"Ask if you can wash your hands while wearing the $39 model."** No waterproofing on their $39 tier. Ours has IPX7.

5. **"Ask about their return/refund process."** Multiple reports of difficult returns and unresponsive support.

6. **"Ask why they added a step counter to Jood Pro."** It signals they're drifting away from pure faith focus toward generic health wearable territory. Is that what you want from a prayer ring?
