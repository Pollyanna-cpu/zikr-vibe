Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 4, 2026

Zikr Vibe ("we", "our", "the app") is a dhikr counter application built by Soul Vibe Technology Limited, Hong Kong. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Our Core Principle

Your dhikr counts are private. Always. We never collect, store, or transmit your dhikr counts, prayer habits, or spiritual practice data to our servers. All counting data stays on your device.

2. Data We Collect

Data stored only on your device (never sent to us):

  • Dhikr counter values and history
  • Streak tracking data
  • App preferences and settings

Data stored on our servers (Supabase) only if you create an account:

  • Email address (for authentication)
  • Display name (chosen by you)
  • Daily presence: a simple yes/no record of whether you used the app on a given day — no counts, no details
  • Group membership (if you join a Dhikr Circle)
  • Notification preferences

Data we never collect:

  • Precise location (we request approximate location only for prayer time calculation, processed on-device)
  • Contacts, photos, or files
  • Device identifiers for advertising
  • Usage analytics or behavioral tracking

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Authentication: To let you sign in and sync across devices
  • Dhikr Circles: To show your circle members that you were present today (not how much you counted)
  • Prayer times: To calculate accurate prayer times for your location (calculated on-device)
  • Notifications: To send prayer reminders and circle nudges, if you opt in

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. We do not use advertising SDKs. We do not integrate with any data brokers.

5. Data Storage and Security

Account data is stored on Supabase (hosted on AWS) with Row Level Security enabled. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the app settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Zikr Vibe is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Your Rights

  • Access: You can view all data we store about you in the app
  • Deletion: You can delete your account and all data from the Profile screen
  • Portability: You can export your data at any time
  • No account required: You can use the app without creating an account — all features work locally

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
